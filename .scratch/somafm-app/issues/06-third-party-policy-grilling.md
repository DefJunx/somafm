# 06 SomaFM terms vs. this app

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

SomaFM's terms (see ticket 02) forbid third-party clients that use their streams, artwork and metadata, even noncommercial, and are not approving new ones. How does this effort proceed? Options: personal-use only and never distributed; ask SomaFM for permission anyway; build a generic radio tuner where SomaFM is one user-added station and nothing is bundled; drop or redirect the project. The answer redraws scope (distribution, bundled artwork, branding, packaging) and may redraw the destination.

## Answer

Personal use only, never distributed (the user never planned to distribute). Consequences: no public releases, installers, app-store listings or published binaries; do not bundle or commit SomaFM artwork, branding or a station snapshot in the repo, fetch at runtime; keep the repo private or at least free of SomaFM assets; no use of SomaFM's name as the app's branding beyond a plain descriptive label.

2026-10-21 update: the "never distributed" stance changed; releases are published on GitHub as a personal passion project.
