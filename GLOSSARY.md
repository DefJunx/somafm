# Glossary

**Station**: A SomaFM radio channel (e.g. Groove Salad), shown as one tile in the grid.

**Grid**: The home screen listing every Station as an image tile.

**Tile**: One Station's card in the Grid; hovering it reveals a play control.

**Player screen**: The full-screen view for the currently selected Station, with transport and volume controls and the now-playing track.

**Active station**: The Station currently playing; it stays marked in the Grid while the user is back on it.

**Now playing**: The track currently broadcast on a Station.

**Favourite**: A Station the user has starred for quick access.

**Mirror**: One of a Station's interchangeable stream servers listed in its playlist; if one fails, another can be tried.

**Theme**: The app's single dark look, derived from SomaFM's palette (gold headings and links, teal accent) without copying its assets. There is no light variant.

**Skeleton tile**: A placeholder Tile shown in the Grid while Stations are still loading.

**Status badge**: The small marker on a Tile showing whether its Station is playing, paused or in error.

**Station history**: The recent tracks a Station has played, as listed by SomaFM's per-Station songs endpoint and shown under the Now playing track on the Player screen.

**Liked track**: A track the user has hearted, stored as its raw "artist - title" string plus the Station it played on and when it was liked; shown in the Liked view.

**Fade**: A short gain ramp applied to the audio when playback starts, stops or switches Station, so the change is not abrupt.

**Visualizer**: The small live bar spectrum of the audio shown on the Player screen while a Station is playing.
